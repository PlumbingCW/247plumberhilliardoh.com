About Hilliard 24/7 Plumbing

A dispatch line for Hilliard, Ohio that is answered at any hour, with plain explanations and a clear price before any work starts.

Call (614) 656-1470

Hilliard 24/7 Plumbing exists for the moment when something is wrong with the plumbing and you need a person to answer the phone. The number is answered around the clock. A dispatcher listens to what is happening, tells you what to shut off, books the earliest appointment available and sends a plumber.

What we do and what we do not

  • ✓We dispatch plumbers for emergencies and planned work in Hilliard and Dublin, Ohio
  • ✓We explain the cause and give a clear price before work starts
  • ✓We offer same-day appointments when available, and we do not promise an exact arrival time
  • ✓We say plainly when a job needs a different trade, such as foundation repair

How a call works

You call, and the dispatcher asks what you see, hear and smell. For a leak, you are told how to find the main shutoff. For a gas smell, you are told to leave the building and call the gas utility first. Then the plumber is scheduled. The first visit is for diagnosis, so the price is based on the real cause.

After the plumber confirms the problem, you receive a price before work starts. If the repair is large, you can pause and decide, and the explanation stays on record. Work is finished, the area is cleaned and the plumber walks you through what was done.

Local rules we follow

The City of Hilliard states that plumbing work needs a permit, and that every contractor working in the city must be registered with the Building Department. You can confirm a contractor's registration through Hilliard 311. Franklin County Public Health provides plumbing inspection for most of Franklin County, with cities such as Columbus and Grove City handled separately. We confirm what applies to your address before the work starts.

What this page does not say

We do not list a founder, a staff roster, years in business or customer counts, because a number or a name here would have to be real and checkable. What you can check is the permit and registration status of the contractor who comes to your door, through the City of Hilliard.

Why a call-only line

There is no form on this website, on purpose. A form sends your problem into a queue that nobody is watching at three in the morning. A phone call gets a person who can ask whether the water is off, who can hear a running toilet in the background and who can tell you what to do in the next five minutes. For a leak, those five minutes decide how much damage there is.

What we ask of you

A good first visit depends on a few details. Know where your main water shutoff is, and tell the dispatcher if it will not turn. Say whether anyone is elderly, ill or unable to leave the house, because that changes what counts as urgent. Tell us about pets, locked gates and parking. If you rent, tell us, because the landlord may need to approve the work, and the dispatcher can explain what the plumber needs from them.

If you have water on the floor, call the line first.

Code references for this work

Our crews work to the plumbing code that applies in Ohio, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and to the National Electrical Code (NEC) wherever a repair touches wiring.
