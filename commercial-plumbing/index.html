Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Hilliard, OH

Businesses cannot wait for the next weekday. We answer after-hours calls, isolate the problem and explain the repair before work starts.

A flooded restroom, an overflowing floor drain or a broken water line in a commercial building is an emergency, because it can close the business for the day. Our line is answered 24/7. While you wait, shut off the water at the main or the nearest isolation valve, keep customers and staff away from the wet area and take photos for your insurer and landlord.

After-hours calls: what a business should do first

  • ✓Close the nearest shutoff valve and the main if needed
  • ✓Keep customers away from wet floors and electrical equipment
  • ✓Tell the dispatcher whether you are the owner, a tenant or a manager
  • ✓Contact the property manager if the building has one
  • ✓Note whether the water is clean or sewage

The question of who authorizes the work matters in a commercial space. Tenants in a leased unit may need the landlord's approval for repairs inside the walls, while the owner of a standalone building can decide on the spot. Tell the dispatcher so the visit goes smoothly.

Restaurants and grease lines

Restaurants have the most frequent plumbing calls in a commercial district. Grease from fryers, dishwashers and prep sinks coats the lines and clogs them from the grill outward. Regular hydro jetting and a grease trap that is cleaned on schedule prevent most of those backups, which tend to happen during the evening rush.

Retail, offices and strip centers

Cemetery Road is a main commercial corridor on the east side of Hilliard, and the I-270 Outerbelt runs alongside it. Strip centers and offices share building drains, so a clog in one unit can back up into another. We locate the shared line, clear it and use a camera to identify where the blockage started.

Planned work and preventive service

Not every call is an emergency. We handle fixture replacements, water heater changes, drain cleaning schedules and pre-purchase inspections for commercial buildings. A sewer camera inspection before you lease or buy a building shows the condition of the building drain.

Honest limits

Our after-hours service is built for urgent plumbing calls. Large projects like a full tenant build-out or a new restroom core usually need a licensed general contractor and plans, and we will tell you if a job is outside what we dispatch.

Permits and registered contractors

The city's Building Standards Division states that plumbing work needs a permit, and every contractor working in Hilliard must be registered with the Building Department. Franklin County Public Health handles plumbing plan review for commercial work in most of the county, though some cities such as Dublin have their own process, so ask early.
