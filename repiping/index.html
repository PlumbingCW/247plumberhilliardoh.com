Repiping in Hilliard, OH

When the pipes themselves are the problem, patching one leak at a time costs more than replacing the run. We explain what we find and what each option involves.

Call (614) 656-1470

Repiping replaces worn supply piping with new copper or PEX. It is a planned job, not an emergency, though a burst or pinhole leak can bring it forward. Call the line to schedule an assessment, and a dispatcher will book the earliest appointment that fits. If a pipe is actively leaking, close the main first and call the 24/7 line.

Signs your pipes are at the end of their life

  • ✓Rust-brown water that clears after running
  • ✓Falling pressure at several fixtures, not just one
  • ✓Pinhole leaks that keep showing up in different places
  • ✓Green or white crust on joints and fittings
  • ✓Walls or ceilings stained along a pipe run

One leak is a repair. A second and third in a few months is a pattern, and repairing each one costs more than the run that is failing.

What your house probably has

Hilliard's housing grew in waves. The city says three large subdivisions arrived in the 1950s, the 1960s brought connection to Columbus water and sewer, and I-270's completion in the 1980s brought a second wave. Homes from the earlier decades may have galvanized steel supply lines, which corrode from the inside and restrict flow. Later houses are more likely to have copper, with some having polybutylene or other plastic that has its own failure history.

We check the material at the meter, the water heater connections and any open joists. A short look at the pipe, a magnet test and a scratch test usually identify it.

Copper, PEX and how the work goes

PEX is flexible, which lets a crew run new lines through walls with fewer openings. It resists freezing better than rigid pipe, though it is not freeze-proof. Copper is durable and familiar, and it costs more in materials. The choice depends on budget, access and preference, and we explain the trade-offs.

A typical whole-house repipe takes one to a few days. Water is off during the work, usually with a break at night. Drywall access holes are patched, though painting is a separate job. Partial repiping of a single run is shorter and can be done from the basement or crawlspace.

Permits and registered contractors

The City of Hilliard states that plumbing work needs a permit, and every contractor in the city must be registered with the Building Department. The inspection follows the work. Verify any contractor through Hilliard 311 before signing.

What affects the cost of repiping

  • ✓Number of fixtures and floors
  • ✓Material choice, copper versus PEX
  • ✓Access through open ceilings or finished walls
  • ✓Whether the water line and shutoffs are replaced too
  • ✓Permit, inspection and patching of access holes

You get a clear price before work starts. Ask for a written scope that lists what is replaced and what is not, since repiping can be partial.
