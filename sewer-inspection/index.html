Sewer Camera Inspection in Hilliard, OH

A sewer camera shows what is actually in the line, whether that is roots, a crack, a belly or nothing at all.

Call (614) 656-1470

A sewer camera inspection is a planned diagnostic visit, not an emergency repair. A camera on a flexible cable travels through the cleanout, records video and shows the exact location of any defect. It is the best way to learn the condition of a line without digging. Call the line to schedule, and a dispatcher will book the earliest appointment that fits.

When a camera inspection makes sense

  • ✓You are buying a house and want the building drain checked first
  • ✓A drain has clogged more than once in a year
  • ✓Several fixtures back up at once
  • ✓You see wet or sunken spots in the yard over the line
  • ✓You want to confirm a repair or a cleaning worked

How the inspection works

We locate the cleanout, feed the camera and record the full run to the connection. Where possible we measure the distance to each defect, and a locator at the surface marks the spot above ground. You receive the video and a short written summary, and we explain what is urgent and what can wait.

Buying a house in the Hilliard area

Hilliard's housing boom ran from the 1950s through the 1980s, and the city connected to the Columbus regional sewer in the 1960s. Houses from that period often have cast iron or clay laterals that are now 40 to 70 years old. A general home inspection rarely looks inside the line. A camera inspection is the step that reveals whether you are buying a clean pipe or a repair bill.

The city runs a Sanitary Backflow Program for homeowners who have had repeated sewer backups into their homes during heavy rain. If the camera shows a defect that matches your backup history, the city's program page is worth a look, and the city asks suspected floor-drain backups to be reported to its Operations Department.

Ownership of the line

Ask the Building Standards Division or the sewer utility where your responsibility ends. A camera inspection can show whether a defect is on your side of the connection.

What affects the price of an inspection

  • ✓Line length and access to a cleanout
  • ✓Whether cleaning is needed before the camera can see
  • ✓Whether a locator and a measured report are included
  • ✓The timing of the appointment

You get a clear price before work starts. If a repair follows, the footage is attached to the quote.

Reading the report and asking the right questions

Ask where each defect sits, how far it is from the cleanout and whether it is likely to get worse. A root intrusion at a joint will return unless the joint is repaired. A belly may be harmless or may hold waste that causes odors and slow drains, depending on its depth. A hairline crack may not need work today but is worth recording. We tell you which items are urgent, which can wait and which need only monitoring, so you can budget instead of guess.
