Sizing a water heater for your household

Size comes from how much hot water you use at the same time, not from the house size. A family that runs a shower, a dishwasher and a washer together needs more recovery than a couple that does not. A tank that is too small runs out. One that is too large wastes energy keeping water hot.

Rough sizing by household
HouseholdCommon tank rangeNotes
1 to 2 people30 to 40 gallonsConsider tankless to save space
3 to 4 people40 to 50 gallonsLook at the first hour rating, not just volume
5 or more, or a large tub50 to 80 gallonsA high recovery gas unit or a tankless may suit better

Those are starting points. We confirm with the fixture count and your habits.

Gas, electric, heat pump or tankless

Gas heats water fast and costs less to run in many homes, but it needs venting and a gas line. Electric tanks are simple and need a 240-volt circuit. Heat pump models use a fraction of the electricity but need open space and air. Tankless units need a sized gas line or a large electric service. Each choice affects what else must be done.

What the install includes

  • ✓Draining and removing the old unit, if there is one
  • ✓New water connections, shutoff valves and flexible lines
  • ✓Venting checked or replaced to suit the unit
  • ✓A relief valve and discharge pipe that meet the manufacturer's requirements
  • ✓An expansion tank where the system is closed
  • ✓A drain pan and, where appropriate, a drain to a floor drain or sump

Expansion tanks and closed systems

When a water meter or a pressure-reducing valve closes the supply side, thermal expansion has nowhere to go. Pressure rises each time the heater cycles, which strains valves and the heater itself. An expansion tank gives the water a place to go. It is a small part with an outsized effect on the life of the heater, and we ask about it on every install.

Check the pressure

If the faucets bang or the relief valve drips after a heating cycle, thermal expansion may be the cause. A pressure gauge on a hose bib tells you the static pressure in minutes.
