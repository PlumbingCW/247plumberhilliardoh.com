Tankless Water Heater Service in Hilliard, OH

Tankless units give endless hot water and take up little space, as long as they are sized, vented and flushed correctly.

Call (614) 656-1470

A tankless water heater heats water as it flows instead of storing it. That saves floor space and removes the risk of a tank failing and flooding the room. It also asks more of the installer, since gas lines, venting and water quality all have to suit the unit. Call the same line we use for emergencies to schedule an install, a repair or a flush.

Is tankless right for your home

Tankless suits households that run out of hot water, homes with limited space and people who want lower standby loss. It is a poor match when the gas line is undersized and cannot be upgraded, or when you rarely use hot water.

Descaling and flushing: the maintenance tankless needs

Columbus reports that it softens water to an average of 120 parts per million, roughly 7 grains per gallon. In moderately hard water, scale forms on the heat exchanger of a tankless heater. It narrows the passages, reduces output and can trigger error codes. A yearly flush with a descaling solution, using the service valves on the unit, prevents most of it.

If your tankless heater has stopped giving steady hot water, shows an error code or has been installed for several years without a flush, a descale visit often restores it. We also check the inlet screen and the venting.

Repairs and error codes

Common faults include a blocked inlet filter, a failed flow sensor, an igniter that does not light and a condensate line that has clogged. Each has a distinct code. We read the code, test the part and explain what we find before replacing anything. If the unit is too old or the heat exchanger is cracked, we say so.

Pairing tankless with a softener

A water softener reduces scale and can help a tankless heater. It is not a substitute for a flush. Read water softener and filtration if you are weighing the two.
