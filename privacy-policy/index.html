Privacy Policy

What we collect when you call or visit this website, and how it is used.

This page explains how Hilliard 24/7 Plumbing handles information. We run a call-only service. This website has no forms, accounts or checkout, so the main information we receive comes from phone calls.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your address, a description of the problem and any details you give the dispatcher. We use it to book the visit, to send the plumber to the right place and to improve the quality of the service.

Website information

Like most websites, the server may log basic technical data, such as the pages visited and the type of device. We use it to keep the site working and secure. This website does not ask you to create an account or enter payment details.

How we use information

  • ✓To answer your call and book the visit
  • ✓To send a plumber to the right address
  • ✓To review calls for quality and training
  • ✓To meet legal and record-keeping requirements

Sharing

We share your details with the plumber assigned to your job so the visit can happen. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing contact@247plumberhilliardoh.com. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

How long we keep information

We keep job records and call recordings only as long as we need them for the job, for quality review and for our legal obligations. After that, they are deleted or made anonymous. You can ask us earlier to delete what we hold about you, and we will tell you if the law requires us to keep any of it.

Children

This website and phone line are for adults who own or manage property. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page. The date at the bottom of the site shows the current year, and the most recent version is always the one on this page.
