Emergency Toilet Repair in Hilliard, OH

A toilet that overflows or leaks at the base is an urgent call. We answer 24/7 and fix the cause rather than the symptom.

If a toilet is overflowing, lift the tank lid, push the flapper down to stop the refill and close the shutoff valve on the wall behind the toilet, turning it clockwise. Put towels around the base and take bathroom rugs away. If the toilet is not the only backing-up fixture, stop using all water and call our 24/7 line, since the problem may be deeper in the drain.

Overflow, leak, rocking: when a toilet cannot wait

A toilet is the one fixture a household cannot go without, and a failing one can damage a bathroom floor in hours. These are the situations that justify an urgent visit.

  • ✓Water spreading around the base after a flush
  • ✓A toilet that overflows repeatedly, even after plunging
  • ✓Sewage smell or water seeping from the floor
  • ✓A cracked bowl or tank
  • ✓A toilet that rocks, which means the seal may be failing

Running and phantom flushing

A toilet that runs on its own wastes water and drives up the bill. The usual causes are a worn flapper that no longer seals, a fill valve that does not shut off and a chain that is too tight or too loose. These are inexpensive parts and the repair is quick. If the fill valve is failing, we will tell you whether to replace the whole assembly or just the part.

Leaking at the base

Water at the base can come from the wax ring seal, the closet bolts, a cracked tank or the supply line. If the toilet rocks, the seal is likely failing. A new wax ring or a sealing gasket and checking that the flange is sound usually resolves it. A damaged flange is a different repair, since a cracked or rusted flange needs to be restored so the toilet can sit flat.

Unclogging without a plunger

If you have no plunger and the bowl is rising, close the shutoff valve and stop flushing. A flange plunger or a toilet auger solves most clogs. Hot tap water and dish soap can loosen a soft blockage over a few minutes. If it keeps returning, the problem may be in the branch line, and drain cleaning is the next step.

Toilet installation and replacement

Replacing a toilet is a fit for the same crews. We remove the old unit, check the flange, install a new wax ring or seal, set the new toilet level and test it. If the floor is soft around the base, we will show you before the new toilet goes in.

Older homes and toilet flanges

In houses built before the 1980s, cast iron and lead closet flanges can corrode. A flange that looks fine from above may be loose or cracked underneath, and a plumber can check it as part of a reset.
