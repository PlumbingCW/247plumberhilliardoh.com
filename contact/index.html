Contact Hilliard 24/7 Plumbing

Call the dispatch line at any hour. There is no form to fill in. A person answers.

Call (614) 656-1470

The fastest way to reach us is the phone. The line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. If water is moving, close the main shutoff before you dial, so the first thing you can tell the dispatcher is that the water is stopped.

Call, email or visit

  • ✓Phone, 24/7: (614) 656-1470
  • ✓Email for non-urgent questions: contact@247plumberhilliardoh.com
  • ✓Dispatch address: 4556 Cemetery Rd, Hilliard, OH 43026

Email is read during the day and is not monitored for emergencies. Do not email about water on the floor. There is no walk-in counter at the dispatch address, so call before you visit.

What to tell the dispatcher

The more specific you are, the better the first visit goes. Here is what helps.

  • ✓Your street address and the nearest cross street
  • ✓What you see, hear or smell, and when it started
  • ✓Whether you have shut off the main water valve
  • ✓Whether anyone is in the house, and whether water is near outlets
  • ✓What you have already tried, such as plunging or a drain cleaner

What happens after you call

The dispatcher books the earliest appointment available and tells you what to do while you wait. We cannot promise an exact arrival time, because it depends on how many urgent calls are ahead and where the crew is. You will get a clear price from the plumber before work starts.

If your problem is not urgent, say so, and the dispatcher will schedule it around your day. Examples include a planned water heater install, a sewer camera inspection and new fixtures.

If you smell gas

A plumbing call is not the first call for a gas smell.

  • !Leave the building right away and take everyone with you
  • !Do not switch lights or appliances on or off
  • !Call the gas utility or 911 from outside
  • !Call us afterward for any plumbing work

If you are calling for someone else

Landlords, property managers and adult children often call for a home they are not standing in. Say so at the start. The dispatcher will ask for the tenant or resident's name and number so the plumber can arrange access, and will ask who approves the work. If the person at the property is elderly, tell the dispatcher, because that changes how urgent the call is and how the first steps are explained.

Calling about a business

For shops, offices and restaurants, tell the dispatcher your hours and whether you can close a section while the plumber works. Say where the main shutoff is and whether a floor drain is involved. The commercial plumbing page explains what an after-hours visit looks like.
