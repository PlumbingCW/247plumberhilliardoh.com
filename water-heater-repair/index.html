Why sediment is a bigger problem in Hilliard

Columbus, which supplies Hilliard properties, reports that it softens its water to an average of 120 parts per million, roughly 7 grains per gallon. That is moderately hard. Minerals precipitate out when water is heated and settle at the bottom of a tank. Over a decade the layer insulates the water from the burner or element, which raises the cost of every shower and shortens the life of the tank.

A yearly flush slows that buildup, and checking the anode rod tells you how much of the tank's protection is left. If the heater is more than ten years old and the rod is gone, we will say plainly that a repair may only buy you a few months, and show you the water heater replacement option beside it.

Gas, electric and tankless: different repairs

Gas heaters rely on a pilot or igniter, a thermocouple and a flue that draws properly. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility before calling us. Electric heaters have two elements and thermostats that fail one at a time, so a heater that runs out of hot water fast may only have lost one element.

Tankless units fail differently. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, and error codes appear. A descale flush often restores them, and the tankless water heater page covers that work.

Hot water and safety

Water above about 120 degrees Fahrenheit can scald in seconds, especially for small children. If you adjust the thermostat yourself, do not set it higher than needed, and let the plumber reset it if you are unsure.

Cost factors and permits

The price depends on the part, how easy the heater is to reach and the time of the call. After-hours dispatch carries a premium.
