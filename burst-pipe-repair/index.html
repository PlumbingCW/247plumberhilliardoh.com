If a pipe has burst, find the main water shutoff and close it now, before you call. The main is usually where the water line enters the house, near the meter in a basement or utility room. Open a faucet at the lowest level to drain what is left in the lines. Then call the 24/7 line, and a dispatcher will tell you what else to do while the crew is on the way.

When a burst pipe is an emergency

It is an emergency whenever water is moving that you cannot stop with a local valve. A split in a supply line can release hundreds of gallons in an hour. Water near a panel, a ceiling light or a ceiling that sags is dangerous. Tell the dispatcher the room, the ceiling condition and whether the main is off.

Why pipes burst in central Ohio

Freeze-thaw winters are the classic cause. Water turns to ice in a pipe and the pressure between the ice and the closed faucet splits it, often a few feet from the freeze. The rupture shows up when the ice thaws, so a quiet pipe in the morning can become a flood by afternoon.

Age is the other cause. Many Hilliard houses date from the 1950s through the 1980s, and by now steel, thin copper and old fittings are wearing out. A pinhole in corroded pipe can grow into a split when pressure surges.

What a repair looks like

  • ✓Locate the break and cut out the damaged section
  • ✓Replace it with new copper or PEX and the right fittings
  • ✓Pressure test the repaired line before it is closed up

A single burst in an older house often means the pipe next to it is not far behind. If the line is galvanized or heavily pitted, we will show you the options, including repiping a section or the whole house.

Frozen pipes that have not burst yet

A pipe that is frozen but intact can be thawed gently. Open the faucet, warm the pipe with a hair dryer on a low setting, and start at the faucet end, never use an open flame. If you cannot find the frozen spot or the pipe is behind a wall, call before it ruptures.

Insurance and documentation

Sudden burst pipe damage is often covered, though deductibles and exclusions differ. Keep photos, any damaged material and a copy of our written findings, and contact your insurer promptly.

Code references for this work

Replacement pipe and fittings must meet the International Plumbing Code (IPC) material rules that Ohio's plumbing code is built on, which is why a plumber will not patch a split with the wrong fitting.
